1. Scooter & Dynamic Range

Pairing a vehicle with a battery automatically calculates the estimated range (e.g. 60V Lithium = 85km vs 72V Lithium = 110km).

3. 1st Charger Included Free

The 1st charger paired with a scooter is automatically ₹0.00. Extra chargers or standalone purchases are billed at standard rate.

4. Smart Auto-Discount

Type your target Final Price (e.g. ₹90,000 against ₹100,000 subtotal) and the system auto-computes the 10% discount and lets you select reasons like Diwali Discount.
